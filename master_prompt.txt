# 13. BOOKING AND RESERVATION PROCESS

The booking system is one of the most important parts of the project.

## Customer booking flow

1. Customer visits the website.
2. Customer explores destinations.
3. Customer selects a destination or package.
4. Customer views price and trip information.
5. Customer selects Book a Trip.
6. Customer logs in or registers if necessary.
7. Customer selects the trip date.
8. Customer selects an available eligible boat.
9. Customer enters the passenger count.
10. Customer enters additional requests.
11. System validates booking information.
12. Customer reviews the booking summary.
13. Customer reads safety guidelines.
14. Customer acknowledges the safety requirements.
15. Customer submits the reservation request.
16. System saves the booking in MySQL.
17. Customer receives a pending-booking notification.
18. Administrator reviews the request.
19. Administrator confirms, rejects, or postpones it.
20. Customer receives a booking status update.

## Required booking fields

- Booking ID
- Customer ID
- Destination/package ID
- Boat ID
- Trip date
- Passenger count
- Additional requests
- Quoted price
- Booking status
- Payment status
- Safety acknowledgment
- Date created
- Date updated

## Booking validation

- Date cannot be in the past.
- Passenger count must be positive.
- Passenger count cannot exceed the boat's certified capacity.
- Boat must be active and eligible for the destination.
- Boat must not already be reserved for an overlapping schedule.
- Destination must be available.
- Customer must be authenticated.
- Required fields must not be blank.
- Extended routes require quotation approval.

Prevent double-booking using database-backed availability checks and transactions.

## Booking statuses

- Pending
- Awaiting Quotation
- Confirmed
- Rejected
- Postponed Due to Weather
- Cancelled
- Completed

The administrator must be able to record a reason for rejection or postponement.

Booking confirmation does not guarantee that the vessel will depart if conditions become unsafe.
